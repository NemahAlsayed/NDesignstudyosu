## 🧩 The "Triple C" Success Formula

To make these prompts work effectively, use this 3-step methodology:

1.  **Role Framing** — Begin prompts by assigning the AI a persona (e.g., *"Act as a senior direct-response copywriter..."* or *"Act as an SEO content specialist..."*).
2.  **Context & Guardrails** — Define target demographics, desired tone (e.g., authoritative, empathetic, witty), word count limits, and required formatting (e.g., bullet points, tables).
3.  **Iterative Refinement** — Treat the initial AI output as a draft. Provide follow-up commands such as *"Make the hook 30% punchier"* or *"Incorporate the phrase [Keyword] naturally in paragraph two."*

> AI isn't here to replace your creativity — it's here to amplify it. By mastering these prompts, you're architecting a strategy that aligns with search algorithms and human psychology.
